# Career context

Material that application answers keep needing but a resume can't hold. Fill in what's useful and leave the rest — this is a working file, not a form. Add to it whenever something comes up that you find yourself explaining again.

Everything here stays local. It's read when running your mock interview and never leaves your machine. This is the same file the `job-application-answers` and `job-application-video-intro-script` skills use — if you already have one, this skill reuses it.

---

## Projects worth telling in full

The resume gives each of these one line. An interview needs the story. Two or three is plenty — pick the ones you'd actually want to be asked about.

For each:
- **What it was** and why it mattered to the business
- **What you specifically did** (not the team — you)
- **What was genuinely hard**, and the tradeoff you chose
- **What happened**, with a number if there is one
- **What you'd do differently**

## Your career story

How you'd explain your path out loud — the through-line connecting what look like separate jobs.

Include anything a screener would otherwise have to ask about: a pivot, a gap, a short tenure, a nonlinear jump. Written plainly and without apology, so it can be dropped into an answer when relevant.
